Your data & privacy

How the AI uses and protects your information

What the AI can access

Transaction history, account balances, scheduled bills, and savings goals within your First Bank account. It cannot access accounts at other institutions.

How long data is retained

AI conversation history is kept for 30 days to improve your experience. You can delete it at any time.

Data sharing

Your financial data is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. It is used solely to generate your personalized insights.

Delete your personalization data

You can reset your profile and delete all personalization data at any time from Settings › Privacy › Clear AI data.
