Transforming the End-to-End EF Customer Experience

Creating seamless customer journeys and aligning internal operations to drive bookings

Timeline
6 months (2019)
My Role
Lead UX Designer & Researcher
Impact
25% increase in conversions

Overview

EF Education First needed to transform their customer experience to better serve families booking language programs abroad. The existing journey was fragmented, with disconnected touchpoints that confused customers and created internal operational challenges.

I led the end-to-end redesign of the customer experience, from initial research through final implementation, working closely with stakeholders across marketing, sales, and operations.

Video I co-produced based on the research

Video Placeholder (16:9)

Where we began

Our challenges:

  • Fragmented customer journey with multiple disconnected touchpoints
  • Inconsistent messaging across digital and physical channels
  • Internal teams operating in silos without shared customer understanding
  • High drop-off rates during the booking process
  • Limited data on customer pain points and decision-making factors

Our first steps

  • Conducted 25+ customer interviews with parents and students
  • Mapped current state customer journey across all touchpoints
  • Ran workshops with internal stakeholders to align on goals
  • Analyzed quantitative data from web analytics and CRM
  • Created service blueprints to identify operational gaps

Key insight: Parents wanted reassurance and transparency throughout the journey, while students were motivated by social connection and adventure. Our solution needed to speak to both audiences.
